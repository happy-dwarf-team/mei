**Chinese, Japanese and Korean text in EU4, without the old DLL patch.**

Mei lets the game show, wrap, search and type the text of a Chinese, Japanese or Korean translation mod. It does not translate the game itself, so subscribe to a translation mod as well.

## What you get
- **Text that shows** in menus, tooltips, events and on the map. Translation packs made for the old double-byte patch work as they are.
- **Proper line breaks** between characters, and no more crash on a long paragraph.
- **Fonts included:** where the game's own fonts have no Chinese, Japanese or Korean letters, Mei brings fonts made from Source Han Sans, about 13,000 characters.
- **Search and typing:** the diplomacy country list and the province finder understand characters, pinyin and initials, and text you type or commit with an IME reaches text fields.
- **Tested with** [Chinese Language Mod for 1.37](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2976470733), [Traditional Chinese for 1.37.5](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2437805432) and [Korean Language Mod - stability](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2714934430).

**Not there yet:** the IME candidate window is untested, text fields have no selection or undo, names kept in script files (such as dynamic province names) show wrong, Japanese kanji in Mei's own fonts take Chinese shapes, and Anvil's own panel shows Latin text only.

## You need
- **[Anvil](https://anvil.happydwarf.dev/) 1.8 or newer** in the game folder. Download it once from its website: the Workshop can't install it for you.
- **EU4 1.37.5** on Steam (Windows). The GOG build is not supported yet.
- **A translation mod,** enabled together with Mei in your playset.
- **No old double-byte patch:** remove its version.dll, d3d9.dll and plugins folder from the game folder first, because it and Anvil can't both be there.
- After subscribing, start the game, approve Mei in Anvil (Ctrl+Shift+F12, then Mods) and restart. There is nothing to configure.
